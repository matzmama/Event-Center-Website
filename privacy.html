Privacy policy.

Last updated October 11, 2026

Your privacy matters. This policy describes how information may be handled when you visit the Silver Crest Estates website.

About this website

This website is a portfolio/demo project created by Jasmine Mathisen. Its event schedule, pricing, and venue details are illustrative and may not describe a real operating venue or service.

Information you provide

The booking inquiry form is currently a visual demo only. It is not connected to a form service or backend, and this website does not transmit form submissions to Silver Crest Estates. Do not enter sensitive personal information into the form.

If a working inquiry service is added in the future, this policy should be updated to identify the information collected, how it is used and retained, and how to contact the responsible site operator.

Third-party services

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts and icons from the Font Awesome CDN. When your browser requests these resources, the providers may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser details under their own privacy policies. Social media links take you to external websites; visiting or interacting with those sites is governed by their respective privacy policies.

Cookies and analytics

This static demo does not intentionally set cookies or use analytics. The hosting provider, if any, may process server logs to deliver and protect the website. If analytics, advertising, embedded media, or other tracking tools are added, this policy should be revised accordingly.

Children's privacy

This website is not designed to collect information from children, and its demo inquiry form should not be used to submit personal information.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated if the website or its services change. For questions about this portfolio project, contact Jasmine Mathisen through the contact method provided by the project owner.

This is general informational copy for a portfolio project, not legal advice. Review and adapt it to your actual services, jurisdiction, and data practices before publishing a live business website.
